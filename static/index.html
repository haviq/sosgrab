Advanced — private reels (bring your own cookie)

Private posts (and most Facebook reels) need your login session. Log in to the platform in your browser, then press F12 → Network, refresh the page, click the first request to the site, and copy the whole Cookie request header. It is used for this one extraction and never stored on the server.

Facebook — cookie harus memuat c_user= dan xs=
Instagram — harus memuat sessionid=
TikTok — sessionid_tt= atau sessionid=
YouTube — jarang perlu; pakai bila muncul "confirm you're not a bot"

Links open the platform CDN directly — use your browser's save function if it plays instead of downloading. Only grab content you own or have the rights to use.
